How the handouts are built

One concept at a time

Each topic follows the same path, so you always know where you are:

  • Definition
  • Intuition
  • Formula
  • Common mistakes
  • Worked examples

How to use them

  • Reviewing a course: start with its lecture notes.
  • Checking homework: open the problem sets, where every step is written out.
  • Everything at once: on GitHub, choose Code, then Download ZIP.
